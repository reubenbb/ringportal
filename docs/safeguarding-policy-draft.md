# RingPortal safeguarding and junior data policy (draft)

**Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-11-21. Not legal advice and not yet adopted.**
Items in [square brackets] need a decision. Check it against England Boxing's current safeguarding
policy and with a solicitor or data protection adviser before relying on it.

## 1. Purpose

RingPortal lets England Boxing clubs arrange bouts and sparring. Many boxers are under 18. This policy
says how RingPortal protects them, what clubs must do, and what RingPortal will do when a concern is raised.

## 2. Who is responsible for what

- **Each club** is responsible for its boxers' welfare, for holding parent or guardian consent, and for
  following England Boxing's safeguarding policy and codes of conduct. Each club's Welfare Officer remains
  the first point of contact for concerns about that club's boxers.
- **RingPortal** is responsible for keeping boxer information secure, limiting what other clubs can see,
  acting on reports, and removing clubs that put boxers at risk.
- **RingPortal's safeguarding lead:** [name, role, email, phone].

## 3. What RingPortal does by design (already built)

- Only clubs verified as England Boxing members can see other clubs or their boxers.
- Other clubs never see a boxer's date of birth, medical status or the club's notes. They see age,
  sex, weight, record and availability.
- For under-18s, other clubs see first name and surname initial only.
- A club cannot add or save an under-18 until it confirms it holds parent or guardian consent. Who
  confirmed, and when, is recorded.
- Under-18s are only suggested as matches for other under-18s.
- Boxers have no accounts and cannot be contacted through RingPortal. All messages are club to club.
- No photos of boxers can be uploaded. Club logos only.
- The public calendar never shows boxers.
- When a boxer transfers, the old club's notes and consent record do not move; the new club must confirm
  consent itself before the boxer can be made available.
- Clubs can report a conversation and block another club. Reports are reviewed by an administrator and
  each review is logged.

## 4. What clubs must do

1. Get written consent from a parent or guardian before listing anyone under 18, and keep it on file.
   [Decide whether RingPortal should supply a standard consent form.]
2. Tell the boxer and their parent or guardian what is listed and who can see it.
3. Record only the medical information needed to arrange bouts safely. Do not record diagnoses or
   details beyond fitness to box.
4. Never put a child's contact details, school, home address or social media in notes or messages.
5. Remove or archive a boxer promptly when they leave the club or consent is withdrawn.
6. Keep the club login to authorised adults who hold a current [DBS check / England Boxing registration].
   [Decide the exact requirement.]
7. Report any safeguarding concern through the club's Welfare Officer and England Boxing's procedures.
   RingPortal's report button does not replace them.

## 5. Raising a concern with RingPortal

- **Immediate danger:** call 999.
- **Concern about a child:** follow England Boxing's safeguarding reporting procedure, or contact the local
  authority children's services or the NSPCC helpline. [Insert current contact details after checking them.]
- **Concern about behaviour on RingPortal:** use "Report" on the conversation, or email [safeguarding email].

RingPortal will acknowledge a report within [2 working days], may suspend a club while it looks into it,
will share information with England Boxing or statutory agencies where that is needed to protect a child,
and will keep a record of what was done.

## 7. Requests from boxers and parents

A boxer or parent can ask their club, or RingPortal, to see, correct or remove the boxer's information.
RingPortal will pass a request to the club the same working day and will remove the record itself if the
club does not act within [14 days].
